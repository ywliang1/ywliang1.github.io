OncRef: A Pan-Cancer PRISM Reference of Cancer Therapeutics
M. G. Rees, M. J. Emmett, …, Y. Liang, …, J. A. Roth, W. R. Sellers (11 of 24 authors) · May 2026
In review at Cell
OncRef is a pan-cancer pharmacological reference built by screening 265 clinically relevant agents, including small molecules, protein degraders, and biologics, against the PRISM collection of 891 barcoded, deeply characterized cancer cell lines spanning 29 lineages. Most inhibitors produced effects consistent with genetic inactivation of their annotated targets, and the strength of these associations frequently tracked with differential clinical trial outcomes. The study also introduces a framework for quantifying specificity, selectivity, on-target polypharmacology, and non-target effects, revealing unintended targets and mediators of toxicity, and establishes a scalable benchmark for evaluating new oncology targets and modalities.

Representation Efficiency in Neural Networks: Evidence in Multilingual Mathematical Benchmarks
Oct 2025 – Dec 2025 · GitHub
Investigated how representation choice affects neural reasoning efficiency in Transformers by multilingual mathematical benchmarks. Built a comprehensive evaluation framework testing SOTA models and 8B-parameter open-source models on GSM8K and MMATH across multiple languages. Discovered that well-trained models achieve representation-invariant reasoning, and when tokenizers align with denser representations, 510% token efficiency gains can be achieved. Demonstrated that representation bottlenecks stem from training distribution rather than architecture by reducing English-Chinese performance gap through LoRA fine-tuning. Developed modular Python infrastructure featuring multitokenizer analysis pipeline and automated evaluation across API and local models.
